# Geo Weather - Nasr City

Geo Weather dashboard for Nasr City weather impact, safe routing, and urban heat risk visualization.

---

## Capabilities
* **Weather-Impact Assessment:** Live meteorological checks via Open-Meteo API.
* **Safe Routing Calculation:** Safety-aware Dijkstra calculation routing transit vehicles away from localized precipitation flood/delay hazards.
* **Urban Heat Islands:** Landsat-derived Celsius surface anomalies explained through land use ratios and built-up compactness indexes.
